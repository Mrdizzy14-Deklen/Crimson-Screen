===Documentation===


		=Chips=
			The chips are the different demons you put on the grid.

			They each have functions that can affect the particle's 
			movement, value, and speed.

			Chips can be dragged between spots but can be locked to 
			rows and collumns if needed.

			Chips have mutliple values, the most important being its 
			functions. The functions are what actually interacts with 
			particles.


		=Particles=


	== Ideas ==
		
		- Demons cost an amount of points
		- You can get specialized demons if you have 'exactly' a 
		  specific amount, or are within a range
		- Some demons are cold and require more use to stay at a healthy temp
